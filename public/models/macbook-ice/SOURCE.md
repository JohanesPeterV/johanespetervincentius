# MacBook in ice

Copied from `Downloads/Igloo-Ice-Models/MacBook-Ice`.

- `macbook-in-ice.glb`: supplied Space Black 16-inch M4 Max MacBook Pro, frozen in its open-lid pose inside a textured ice shell.
- `studio.exr`: source environment lighting.

MacBook geometry comes from the owner's supplied Blender model. Ice geometry, normal/roughness maps, and environment come from Igloo Inc. public assets (https://www.igloo.inc/). Source artwork and brand marks retain their original attribution. The source website's custom refraction and mouse-frost shaders are not included.

Editable Blender files, rebuild scripts, and validation records remain in the Downloads source package.
