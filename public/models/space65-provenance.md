## Integration Notes

The asset is Y-up: keycaps face +Y, the spacebar is toward +Z and the number row toward -Z. Rest bounds are approximately 0.330425 x 0.034510 x 0.116040 metres (X/Y/Z). Liners, legends and homing bars remain children of their key shells and follow key translation without separate animation tracks.

Loading requires Meshopt decoding. Preserve the physical materials and their `KHR_materials_transmission`, `KHR_materials_volume`, `KHR_materials_clearcoat`, `KHR_materials_specular` and `KHR_materials_emissive_strength` properties. The source has no explicit `KHR_materials_ior`; effective IOR remains the glTF default 1.5. Shells and homing bars are single-sided, alpha-opaque transmission materials, not alpha-blended glass. Their transmission is approximately 0.96, thickness 0.0005 m and attenuation distance 0.000625 m. Shell roughness factor is 1 with the embedded roughness map; the untextured homing bars use approximately 0.35. Keep the separate white liners opaque and white, and preserve material names, texture bindings and authored optical scale when integrating.

`Space65Keyboard` loads the delivery through Drei's bundled Meshopt decoder and normalizes its width to 3.3 world units. Instance-owned material clones preserve the supplied body, shells, opaque liners, textures and optical parameters. Only LED colors follow the active theme; their authored emissive strengths remain intact. The original typing animation and reduced-motion resting pose are retained.

Integrated desktop/mobile checks cover material bindings and white liners, chapter selection, vertical exit and return, light/dark themes, reduced motion, and failed-load recovery.
