# Review Focus

Updated: 2026-07-22

This file tells reviewer agents what matters most now. It prioritises review; it does not turn preferences into findings without current code, rulebook, or reproducible runtime evidence.

## Primary Outcome

Make `/igloo-preview` feel as smooth, legible, expensive, and whole as possible while preserving the accepted world-rise concept. Timing, restraint, and frame stability matter more than additional spectacle.

## Prioritise

- Continuous motion and readable pacing across the full loop.
- A quiet, nearly fixed camera while the world rises.
- Stable frame time and graceful degradation on weaker hardware.
- Restrained effects that support rather than hide the transition.
- Clear composition, focal objects, copy, and interaction at desktop and mobile sizes.

## Protect

- The contracts and Definition of Done in `IGLOO-PREVIEW.md`.
- Scroll down means objects rise while the camera barely moves.
- The W1 reveal, transition seam, narrative stones, and finale remain readable.
- Existing repository code-shape, hook, file-size, and reuse rules.

## Out Of Scope

- Reopening comparison or parity work with `igloo.inc` unless the user explicitly asks.
- Sourced or generated art, literal igloo elements, or new portfolio content.
- New animation libraries or unrelated general refactors.
- Extra visual detail used to compensate for weak timing or motion.

## Evidence Bar

Queue a finding only when it is supported by at least one of:

- A violated repository or feature invariant.
- A reproducible browser, accessibility, responsive, console, or performance problem.
- A concrete code-shape problem that meaningfully harms correctness or maintainability.

If this focus becomes stale or conflicts with a newer direct user instruction, follow the newer instruction and update this file in the same task.
